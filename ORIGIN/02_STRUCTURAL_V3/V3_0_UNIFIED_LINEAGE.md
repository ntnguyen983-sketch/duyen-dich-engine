# DUYÊN DỊCH 3.0 — UNIFIED STRUCTURAL LINEAGE CAPTURE

## Constitutional continuity
v3.0 preserves Đạo–Duyên–Dịch: Duyên is a stable pattern in a condition network above a transition density; Dịch is transformation as underlying conditions and force relations change. Quẻ/network are snapshots, not deterministic fate.

## Structural role
v3.0 is the bridge from the earlier DCGF ontology into a more unified architecture. It treats observed conditions as a graph and retains the separation between raw observation, structural representation, dynamic computation, evaluation and interpretation.

## Ontology direction
The source explicitly generalizes Duyên beyond a fixed physical object: concepts, people and entities may participate in the Dòng. This is the historical precursor to the later N(n) / Entity–Observation–Interaction–State–Time–Space architecture.

## Lineage role
This file is a historical capture only. It does not declare which v3.0 formulations remain canonical after later 3A work. Conflicts must be resolved only after the source corpus is fully assembled.
