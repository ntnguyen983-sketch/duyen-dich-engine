# MASTER D.D. — EARLY DAO–DUYEN–DICH LINEAGE CAPTURE

Source: `Master D.D.pdf` (Library source captured 2026-08-21).

## Core ontology
Dòng is the continuous motion of the network of Duyên under Lực, Hướng and Thế. Chuyển hóa is the process by which Duyên changes state, dissolves, aggregates, combines, separates or forms new interaction structures. Quẻ is a compressed Snapshot of Dòng at a point in time.

## Lifecycle of Duyên
Hiện diện → Tham gia Dòng → Tương tác → Sinh Lực/Hướng/Thế → Chuyển hóa → Tiếp tục tham gia/Rời Dòng → Trở thành lịch sử.

A Duyên remains active while it participates in the Dòng, creates/receives interactions, retains influence, has not fully transformed, and remains phase-aligned. It becomes historical when it no longer participates/interacts, its force dissipates, its direction moves to another flow, it transforms completely, or loses phase alignment.

## Pipeline
S00–S06: collect signals, initialize Duyên, compute V_khi and construct SIE graph.
S07–S09: semantic interpretation / Tượng layer and emergent Tượng.
S10: resolve conflicts by priority axis.
S11–S12: multi-level verification, standardized seven-part output and self-correction loop.

## Output principle
Identity_ID is a trace/reference identifier and does not represent an independently existing entity. The four-state tuple captured in the source is H (Direction), L (Force), K (State/Qi), T (Context). Relation_Edges and Momentum_Value preserve network dynamics.

## Historical significance
This source preserves the early conceptual formulation in which Duyên is explicitly conditional and processual rather than a permanent object, and the Quẻ is a Snapshot of a changing Dòng. It is kept as historical source material without reconciliation.
